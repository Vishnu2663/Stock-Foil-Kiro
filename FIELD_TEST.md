# Field test checklist

The thresholds and the 1.2 mm punched-together tolerance come from the simulator. Test them with real paper, punches and phones before relying on them. Record results in a copy of this file.

## Print

- [ ] Print a card at 100% (actual size) on plain A4, 80 g/m². Measure: each half's frame is 186 × 120 mm, corner squares are 7 mm.
- [ ] Print the same card from the downloaded PDF. Same measurements.
- [ ] Print one card with "Printed grey discs" on. The discs look evenly grey.

## Cut and punch

- [ ] Cut on the wavy line with scissors. Try one careful cut and one sloppy cut.
- [ ] Stack the halves and punch 6 to 10 days with a standard 6 mm office punch.
- [ ] Punch one extra hole on the stock only (should show as disputed, never paid).
- [ ] Punch one day separately on each half, about 2 mm off (should ask for a review).

## Scan (at least 3 phones: one low-end Android, one mid-range Android, one iPhone)

For each phone, on a dark cloth, a pale table and a white table:

- [ ] Camera opens on HTTPS, the torch button appears where supported.
- [ ] Both halves are captured automatically. Overlay lines up with the corner squares and circles.
- [ ] The punched days are exactly right, or the uncertain ones are dashed amber. Never a confident wrong day.
- [ ] Normal cards on a white table warn "Place the card on a dark plain surface".
- [ ] Disc cards read on dark and white surfaces; a mid-grey surface marks every circle unsure.
- [ ] Photos taken from far away, blurred, or with a corner cut off warn instead of guessing.
- [ ] The sloppy cut still verifies (pairing code). A half from another card is rejected.
- [ ] Note the scan time per half: ___ s.

## Calibrate

- [ ] Upload photos of the punched cards on the Calibrate page. Note the suggested threshold and tolerance.
- [ ] Apply them, rescan, then Reset to defaults. Nothing changed before Apply.

## Pay, voice and records

- [ ] Mark as paid, scan again: "Already paid on <date>".
- [ ] Pictures-and-voice mode: the result is read aloud in the worker's language. Note voice quality per language.
- [ ] A native speaker reviews each language pack (hi, bn, ta, es, sw) and the Story.
- [ ] Turn on airplane mode after one visit: every page still opens.
